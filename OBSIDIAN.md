# Obsidian: The A-LAB Knowledge Vault

Welcome to the **A-LAB.TECH Knowledge Engine**. This vault is where our Roadmaps, Whitepapers, and R&D notes transform into neural assets.

## 🌉 Connecting the Ecosystem

### 1. The Pinokio Link
You can now open this vault directly from your Pinokio dashboard. Look for the **"Obsidian Vault"** icon. Clicking it triggers:
- `obsidian://open?path=/Users/johnsky/Documents/A-lab.tech`
- This ensures your current workspace is always synchronized with your notes.

### 2. The AI-Ollama Bridge
To make Obsidian "smart" using your local **Ollama** (already running in Pinokio), we recommend installing:
- **Plugin: Copilot** (by Logan Yang)
- **Settings**:
  - Provider: **Ollama**
  - Host: `http://localhost:11434`
  - Model: `llama3` or `A_LAB_EXPERT_v1`
- **Result**: You can chat with your Obsidian vault using your local AI-Expert.

### 3. Folder Structure for A-LAB
- `/R&D`: Drafts for future projects and Whitepapers.
- `/Docs`: The core project files (`ROADMAP.md`, `OS_WHITEPAPER_STRUCTURE.md`).
- `/Residents`: Profiles and notes on lab members.
- `/Neural`: Prompts and instructions for AI agents.

## ⚙️ Development Guide
- Use **Wikilinks** (`[[Page Title]]`) to connect R&D ideas.
- Use **Tags** (#rd, #infrastructure, #chameleon-os) to categorize projects.
- Use **Proof-of-Existence (PoE)** hashes in your notes to verify original authorship.
